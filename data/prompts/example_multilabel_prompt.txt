You will receive short interview excerpts.

Task:
Assign one or more codes from the allowed labels that apply to each excerpt.
- Choose all codes that are clearly supported by the excerpt.
- If no code applies, use only "n/a".
- Never combine "n/a" with other codes.

Codebook (label definitions):
- economic_strain:
  Mentions financial pressure or material hardship (e.g., difficulty paying bills, rising costs, rent/food insecurity, job insecurity).
  Exclude: general dissatisfaction without a clear financial component.

- care_responsibilities:
  Mentions unpaid caregiving or care labor (e.g., childcare, elder care, caring for a sick/disabled family member) or responsibility for dependents.
  Exclude: professional caregiving jobs unless personal caregiving burden is explicitly described.

- mental_wellbeing:
  Mentions mental health, stress, anxiety, depression, emotional exhaustion, sleep problems, or coping difficulties.
  Exclude: purely physical health unless psychological impact is clearly stated.

- trust_in_institutions:
  Mentions trust/distrust, confidence, or perceived fairness/competence of institutions (e.g., government agencies, welfare offices, schools, healthcare, police).
  Exclude: complaints about individuals unless linked to an institution or system.

If none of the codes apply, output only: ["n/a"].
